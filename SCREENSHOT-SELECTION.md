Reviewed the two uploaded marketing packages on 10 October 2026. All selected captures show native app UI with original demonstration content. No UI was retouched. Supplied app icons replace the generic book and music symbols throughout navigation, hero links, the catalog, and product titles.

The updated LibraVia package adds a connected synthetic Jellyfin library. Its Home captures now introduce the app, followed by reading, appearance, and contents. The selected Mac PNGs use the package's supplied web crops, which omit 104 pixels of capture chrome and its pointer; no additional retouching was performed.

The lyrics and extra album/reader-theme captures are retained in the original uploaded packages but are not needed for this concise app walkthroughs. Capture dimensions, file names, and hashes of the selected assets are recorded in `assets/screens/capture-info.json`. The 3D screen renderer contains images at their original aspect ratio, including the supplied LibraVia Mac crops.
