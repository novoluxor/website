OUR APPROACH

Own your setup.
Enjoy using it.

We tried the existing clients. Each one left something missing.
So we started building the apps we wanted to use.

01

Start with a real need.

A music player without a Mac app. Controls that are too small. A reader that needs another setup step. These are problems we ran into ourselves.

02

Make the details matter.

Familiar Apple controls, transitions, and layouts. Custom design earns its place only when it makes the experience better.

03

Share what helps.

We build for ourselves first. When a solution feels useful, we share it with others who have the same needs.
